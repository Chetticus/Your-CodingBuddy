The private Python solutions favor explicit loops for teaching and use a slightly different third sample book from the current public download. They implement the same core behavior. Before release, select one canonical final file and align written snippets, captions, recording and downloadable code. Do not silently replace lesson IDs or lose existing progress.

## For every finished lesson collect

- Stable lesson ID and VI/EN title.
- Actual duration from the edited file.
- One-sentence outcome, prerequisites and lesson body.
- Final video file and reviewed VI/EN caption files.
- Starter file, exercise, hints and reference solution.
- A tested download filename and short description.
- Preview/free/paid access decision, reviewed by Nam.

The ten folders currently contain drafts and executable teaching examples, not completed recordings. Do not create placeholder video URLs or mark these as published.

## After Nam reviews the finished content

1. Confirm course grouping, intended audience, price and what personal support is included.
2. Choose video storage/delivery appropriate to the existing player and access rules. Do not promise that videos cannot be copied.
3. Add content in a local or staging environment, preserving course and learner data.
4. Test both languages, captions, downloads, playback on a phone, free previews and enrolled access.
5. Check that paid enrollment still leads to direct contact and that access is granted only through the intended enrollment process.
6. Ask for the separate deployment authorization before uploading or publishing to the live site.

## Editorial checks

- No invented student outcomes, workshop photos, partnerships or donation totals.
- No subtitle file created from estimated timestamps.
- Sample data and demonstration projects labeled clearly.
- Actual availability matches course cards and purchase/contact expectations.
- The older demonstration courses remain a separate editorial review item. This pack does not certify their accuracy or readiness for sale.
