# CodingBuddy — your recording desk

Prepared for Nam · 26 October 2026 · private production drafts

You can start rehearsing the first Python recording now. Nothing in this folder has been published or added to the live course catalog. Scripts are speaking notes to adapt, not statements about teaching you have already done.

## Start with these three steps

1. Read [the recording setup](RECORDING-SETUP.md), then make a 60-second sound-and-screen test.
2. Open [lesson 01](python-path/01/TEACHER.md). Keep the notes off the recorded screen and open its `starter.py` in your editor.
3. Rehearse, record one take, and watch it before recording lesson 02. Update [the production tracker](PRODUCTION-TRACKER.md).
